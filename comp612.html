Snow Scene

Procedural 2D winter scene (freeGLUT)

Development work log

View Snow Scene work log (inline PDF)

Purpose

2D OpenGL scene: hills, sky, snowman, and falling snow with front/back layers.

What I built

  • Curved ground and gradient sky
  • Snowman from simple shapes + optional top hat
  • Particle snow (toggle with S, quit with Q)
  • Wind on arrow keys, hat blocks front-layer flakes

Challenge

Draw order and typos kept hiding the snowman. The HUD only updated once I moved stats to idle().

Approach

Build on Mac, package for Windows. Tune ground, particles, and layers separately.

Result

Snow reads in front and behind the figure; hat collision works. See the work log for details.

Reflection

Plan draw layers on paper first next time; most fixes were ordering, not maths.

Helicopter Scene

3D flight sandbox (OpenGL)

Development work log

View Helicopter work log (inline PDF)

Purpose

3D flight sandbox: camera, lighting, textures, fog, and landing on terrain.

What I built

  • Helicopter mesh, rotors, landing gear
  • Flyable controls with sensible scale (1 unit ≈ 10 m)
  • Depth test, sun + spotlight, optional fog (F)
  • HUD for grounded vs flying; simple scenery

Challenge

Lighting and movement direction were wrong until depth test and trig were fixed. Landing needed a buffer zone, not a hard Y lock.

Approach

Milestone builds: camera first, then flight, lighting, textures, HUD.

Result

Stable flight, grounded landing, readable scene on Mac and Windows.

Reflection

Turn on depth test early; test landing at speed before polishing lights.
